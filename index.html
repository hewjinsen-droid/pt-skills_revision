Shoulder: Flexion

Daniels & Worthingham
https://encrypted-tbn2.gstatic.com/licensed-image?q=tbn:ANd9GcSG5jii0frc3jP3Y32ghvyKAJKSKNknjyp6EjAa3BdYTaYgS0trsRA1HsAG-5H4R2nNoc50HCDUF-MBpLo

Target Muscles: Anterior Deltoid, Coracobrachialis.

Force Direction: Applied to distal humerus in downward direction.

Grades 5, 4, 3 (Against Gravity)

Patient seated. Patient flexes shoulder to 90° without rotation. Therapist applies resistance downward just above the elbow.

Grades 2, 1, 0 (Gravity Eliminated)

Patient side-lying on non-test side. Therapist supports arm. Patient attempts to flex shoulder.

Target Muscles: IR: Subscapularis. ER: Infraspinatus, Teres Minor.

Force Direction: Applied to distal forearm opposing rotational movement.

Grades 5, 4, 3 (Against Gravity)

Patient prone, shoulder abducted to 90°, elbow flexed to 90° off table edge. Patient rotates forearm up (ER) or back and up (IR).

Grades 2, 1, 0 (Gravity Eliminated)

Patient seated or short-sitting, elbow flexed to 90° resting on table. Patient moves forearm in transverse plane.

Transfers: Sit-to-Stand

Standard Frame / Sticks
https://encrypted-tbn2.gstatic.com/licensed-image?q=tbn:ANd9GcSG5jii0frc3jP3Y32ghvyKAJKSKNknjyp6EjAa3BdYTaYgS0trsRA1HsAG-5H4R2nNoc50HCDUF-MBpLo
  • Step 1: Patient scoots forward to the edge of the chair.
  • Step 2: Position the walking aid directly in front (or to the side for crutches/sticks). Ensure brakes are locked if using a rollator.
  • Step 3: Unaffected (strong) leg is placed slightly backward under the chair for leverage. Affected leg is extended slightly forward.
  • Step 4: Hands are placed on the armrests of the chair (DO NOT pull up on the walking frame).
  • Step 5: Lean trunk forward ("nose over toes") and push up from the chair.
  • Step 6: Once upright and balanced, transfer hands one at a time to the walking aid grips.

Transfers: Stand-to-Sit

Standard Frame / Sticks
https://encrypted-tbn2.gstatic.com/licensed-image?q=tbn:ANd9GcSG5jii0frc3jP3Y32ghvyKAJKSKNknjyp6EjAa3BdYTaYgS0trsRA1HsAG-5H4R2nNoc50HCDUF-MBpLo
  • Step 1: Approach the chair and slowly turn around.
  • Step 2: Back up until the back of the knees touch the edge of the seat.
  • Step 3: Extend the affected leg slightly forward.
  • Step 4: Release hands from the walking aid one at a time, reaching backward to firmly grasp the chair's armrests.
  • Step 5: Control the descent using the arms and the unaffected leg, lowering slowly into the seat.

Gait Patterns

FWB, PWB, NWB
https://encrypted-tbn2.gstatic.com/licensed-image?q=tbn:ANd9GcSG5jii0frc3jP3Y32ghvyKAJKSKNknjyp6EjAa3BdYTaYgS0trsRA1HsAG-5H4R2nNoc50HCDUF-MBpLo

Full Weight Bearing (FWB / WBAT)

  • Move the walking aid forward.
  • Step forward with the affected leg.
  • Step past with the unaffected leg (normal heel-strike to toe-off).

Partial Weight Bearing (PWB)

  • Advance the walking aid and the affected leg simultaneously.
  • Bear partial weight down through the hands onto the aid.
  • Step through with the unaffected leg.

Non-Weight Bearing (NWB / 3-Point Gait)

  • Affected leg must remain off the ground entirely.
  • Advance the walking aid forward.
  • Push heavily downward through the hands.
  • Hop forward with the unaffected leg to the level of the aid.

Stairs Negotiation

Crutches / Sticks
https://encrypted-tbn2.gstatic.com/licensed-image?q=tbn:ANd9GcSG5jii0frc3jP3Y32ghvyKAJKSKNknjyp6EjAa3BdYTaYgS0trsRA1HsAG-5H4R2nNoc50HCDUF-MBpLo

Ascending ("Good leg to heaven")

  • Stand close to the bottom step.
  • Bear weight through the hands/aid.
  • Step up with the unaffected (good) leg first.
  • Follow by bringing the affected leg and the walking aid up to the same step.

Descending ("Bad leg to hell")

  • Stand near the edge of the step.
  • Lower the walking aid and affected leg down to the step below simultaneously.
  • Bear weight heavily through the hands.
  • Step down with the unaffected (good) leg to join the affected leg.
